## Objective
Retail sales data ka exploratory analysis karke sales patterns, customer behaviour aur business insights nikalna.

## Dataset
- Retail Sales Dataset (Kaggle, mohammadtalib786)
- 1,000 rows, 9 columns: Transaction ID, Date, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Product Category, Quantity, Price per Unit, Total Amount

## Tools Used
Python, pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn, Jupyter Notebook (VS Code)

## What I Did
1. Data inspection: shape, dtypes, null values, duplicates
2. Descriptive statistics: mean, median, mode, std deviation
3. Time series: monthly and quarterly sales trend
4. Customer analysis: age groups and gender breakdown
5. Product analysis: revenue and quantity by category
6. Correlation heatmap of numerical variables
7. Extra insight: age group vs category sales heatmap, gender-wise category preference
